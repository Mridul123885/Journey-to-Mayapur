Privacy Policy — Journey to Mayapur

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the "Journey to Mayapur" mobile application ("the App") handles information.

Summary

The App does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, or share any personal information. Everything the App saves stays on your own device.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require account creation or sign-in. The App does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. The App does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services of any kind. The App does not connect to any external server or database to store your data.

Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves the following information locally on your own device only, using standard Android app storage:

  • Your progress in the app's games (unlocked stages, scores, achievements)
  • Your chanting/reading tracker entries, if you use those features
  • Your notification reminder preferences (which reminders are enabled and at what times)
  • General app settings (such as sound on/off)

This information is never transmitted anywhere. It stays on your device and is only accessible to the App itself. If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted along with it.

Notifications

If you enable reminders within the App, the App schedules local notifications directly through your device's operating system. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device — no notification content or scheduling data is sent to any external server.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be family-friendly and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated in the future, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
